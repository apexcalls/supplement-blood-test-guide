Should You Get a Blood Test Before Choosing a Supplement?

Many supplement decisions are made on guesswork: low energy, poor sleep, or a vague sense of deficiency. A blood test replaces guesswork with numbers. It can show whether a genuine deficiency exists, whether levels are already adequate, and whether a supplement is actually changing anything over time. Without a baseline, there is no way to know whether a product is helping or simply expensive.

Blood tests are not a cure-all. Reference ranges are broad, some markers fluctuate daily, and a single result rarely tells the full story. But as a starting point, a test beats a marketing claim. If a supplement is meant to correct a deficiency, knowing your level before and after is the only honest way to judge it.
